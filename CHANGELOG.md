# FlashCD 1.0.0

The first release.

FlashCD shows a spell's icon in the middle of your screen the moment its cooldown is ready. It tracks your spells with a cooldown on its own from the first login, so it works without any setup.

What's in it:

- Pick which spells and items flash, each with its own sound, glow colour and group if you like.
- Up to ten groups, each with its own size, place and look. Drag them into place, nudge them with the arrows and scroll to resize.
- Presets to start from, with a preview before you pick one.
- An optional "Soon" warning a few seconds before a cooldown is ready.
- Flash only in combat, only in a form, or skip short cooldowns.
- Share your settings as a line of text, or copy them between your characters.
- A Recent flashes page that tells you why something didn't flash.
- A welcome window the first time you install it, and two looks for the settings window.
