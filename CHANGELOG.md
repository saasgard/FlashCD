# FlashCD 1.0.1

- Profiles: the Profiles page now lists this character's profile by name, your other characters' and copies you save yourself. Save a copy, load one onto any character, or delete copies you no longer need.
- Loading a profile puts your spells back in the groups they had in it, also when you don't load the tracked spells.
- Undo: put your settings back after loading a profile, importing, picking a preset or starting fresh.
- "Flash in" for each spell: any time, in any form, or only in one form (say, only in Cat Form). Spells set to "only in a form" before keep working.
- Groups can be moved up and down in the list.
- The Profiles page scrolls, so nothing is cut off in a small window or the WoW Forever theme.
- Switching between the Modern and WoW Forever looks no longer builds a new window each time.
- The Test buttons on a group's page and in the spell panel now flash in front of the settings window instead of behind it.
- Previewing a preset shows one spell per group, so it's easier to see what you're picking.

# FlashCD 1.0.0

The first release.

FlashCD shows a spell's icon in the middle of your screen the moment its cooldown is ready. It tracks your spells with a cooldown on its own from the first login, so it works without any setup.

What's in it:

- Pick which spells and items flash, each with its own sound, glow colour and group if you like.
- Up to ten groups, each with its own size, place and look. Drag them into place, nudge them with the arrows and scroll to resize.
- Presets to start from, with a preview before you pick one.
- An optional "Soon" warning a few seconds before a cooldown is ready.
- Flash only in combat, only in a form, or skip short cooldowns.
- Share your settings as a line of text, or copy them between your characters.
- A Recent flashes page that tells you why something didn't flash.
- A welcome window the first time you install it, and two looks for the settings window.
